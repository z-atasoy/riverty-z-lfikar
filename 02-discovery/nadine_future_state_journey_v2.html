Nadine's future state: one consistent way to pay

Strategy: one consistent way to pay that feels the same online and in a store, with fewer payment decisions.

Nadine, 38, frequent online shopper. Uses three or more payment methods every month.

Value prop: shop without worrying whether Riverty is listed at checkout, and build loyalty that carries into physical purchases.

  1. 1

    Set up

    Card in her wallet at approval

    User action

    • Add approved card to phone wallet in one tap → Riverty is ready before plastic arrives.
    • Skip activation steps → Card works the moment it’s added.

    Internal state

    Sceptical of another card, but curious if it removes decisions.

    Pain point addressed

    • Use the wallet she already has → no new app or habit. UXR-06, UXR-08, OBS-103
  2. 2

    Online checkout

    Same card, any shop

    User action

    • Pay with the card at any online shop → No reliance on Riverty being listed.
    • Stop comparing checkout options → One decision replaces many.

    Internal state

    Relieved; the shop's payment menu stops mattering.

    Pain point addressed

    • Use the same card everywhere → availability stops deciding. UXR-01, OBS-105
  3. 3

    In store

    Same tap, same routine

    User action

    • Tap phone at the till → Same routine as online.
    • Skip remembering what works where → Acceptance wherever Mastercard is taken.

    Internal state

    Confident; no mental check of what this store supports.

    Pain point addressed

    • Close the online versus store gap → payment feels the same. UXR-04, UXR-09, OBS-107
  4. 4

    After purchase

    Clear record, flexibility on request

    User action

    • Review card purchases in one place → Fewer “which provider?” moments.
    • Optionally split a larger purchase → Flexibility on her terms, never pushed.

    Internal state

    In control; Riverty becomes a card she trusts and reaches for.

    Pain point addressed

    • Keep one record of card spend → less forgetting what was paid. UXR-05, UXR-07, UXR-10

Three advantages over today's manual workaround

Workaround today: memory as the tracking system, plus a bank card as the universal fallback.

  1. One consistent card replaces remembering what works where → Fewer decisions at every checkout and till.
  2. A card record replaces memory as the tracker → Fewer forgotten card payments.
  3. Acceptance plus optional flexibility replaces the plain bank-card fallback → Flexibility travels with her.
Based on synthetic, directional research (UXR-01 to UXR-12, OBS-101 to OBS-108). Assumptions to validate: she replaces an existing card habit (7 of 8 participants say a new card must), the single record covers card spend only, and optional splitting is a later release resting on one interview (UXR-10).
