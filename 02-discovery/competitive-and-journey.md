# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Persona 2: Nadine, the Fragmentation-Weary Shopper

(UXR-11, UXR-09, UXR-05, OBS-107)

Role: A 38-year-old frequent online shopper who juggles three or more payment methods every month.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** One consistent way to pay that feels the same online and in a store, with fewer payment decisions.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Standing in a store or at a checkout, she has to remember which method works where, and later may not even recall whether she paid with Klarna, PayPal or her bank. Her line, "I don't need another credit card. I need fewer payment decisions," is the sharpest expression of the problem.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Relies on memory of which method works at which merchant
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** -Check what she will buy
-Think which methods are available in which merchants
-Find the merchants and method matching what she will buy
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** she cannot memorize or keep in mind each and every merchants and payment method combos so missing some opportunities sometimes
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** Evidenced (UXR-09: "having to remember which one works where")
