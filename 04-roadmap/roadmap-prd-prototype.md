# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Nadine, 38, a frequent online shopper who juggles 3 or more payment methods a month. She wants one consistent way to pay, online and in store, with fewer decisions. The friction we confirmed is that she re-decides at every checkout, depends on whether Riverty is listed, and later can't recall which provider paid.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** The card's share of an active cardholder's monthly payments. The pilot baseline is 14%, and the target is about 20%.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Standing in a store or at a checkout, she has to remember which method works where, and later may not even recall whether she paid with Klarna, PayPal or her bank. Her line, "I don't need another credit card. I need fewer payment decisions," is the sharpest expression of the problem.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Riverty checkout GMV at partner merchants among cardholders. It must not fall more than 5% against matched non-cardholders.
