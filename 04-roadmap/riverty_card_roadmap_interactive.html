Riverty Consumer Card: interactive roadmap

Germany · 3 back-end, 1 front-end, 1 QA, 1 UX designer · 2 sprints. Click any feature to see its score and rationale.

Strategic anchors
Persona
Nadine, 38, a frequent online shopper who juggles 3 or more payment methods a month. She wants one consistent way to pay, online and in store, with fewer decisions.
Primary metric
The card's share of an active cardholder's monthly payments. Pilot baseline 14%, target about 20%.
Moment of misery
Standing in a store or at a checkout, she has to remember which method works where, and later may not recall whether she paid with Klarna, PayPal or her bank. “I don't need another credit card. I need fewer payment decisions.”
Guardrail
Riverty checkout GMV at partner merchants among cardholders must not fall more than 5% against matched non-cardholders.
